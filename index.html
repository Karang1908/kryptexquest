    How the quest works

    1. Team up.2 to 4 players. One creates the team, the rest join with its code, then the leader locks it in.
    2. Walk to a glowing stop.Your explorer follows your real GPS position. Drag the map to look around.
    3. Unlock it.Enter the handoff flag from the previous location.
    4. Solve three puzzles.Enter flags, or photograph the real object that's described.
    5. Follow the clue.Three solves reveal the next clue and its password.

    How the quest works

    1. Team up.1 to 4 players: solo is fine. One creates the team, the rest join with its code, then the leader locks it in.
    2. Check in at the base.Walk to the vending machine area. The locations and their hints appear. Some open with a question you answer there.
    3. Walk into a location to unlock it.Locations unlock in order: hand in the previous location's code at the base first. Locked ones show as grey beacons. Indoors with bad GPS? Scan the QR posted at the spot.
    4. Clue, photo, question.Questions in a location open one at a time. Photo questions give a clue to a real object: photograph it and the question appears. Every answer is a flag.
    5. Hand in each location's code at the base.That unlocks the next location. When every code is in you have finished, and your time and rank appear.

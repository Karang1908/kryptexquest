How the quest works

  1. Travel to a stop.Find the marker at the real campus landmark.
  2. Unlock it.Enter the handoff flag from the previous location.
  3. Solve three puzzles.Enter flags or capture the requested real-world object.
  4. Follow the reveal.Three verified flags expose the clue and password for the next stop.

This is a UI prototype. Photo review and flag checks are simulated locally; production validation belongs on a server.
